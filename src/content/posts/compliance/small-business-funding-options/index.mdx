## Small business funding options

-   [Funding options for minority-owned businesses](https://www.uschamber.com/co/run/business-financing/funding-options-for-minority-owned-businesses), including multiple grants, funding sources and low-cost loans that are intended to help minority-owned businesses grow.
-   [Funding options for women-owned businesses](https://www.uschamber.com/co/run/business-financing/funding-options-for-women-owned-businesses), including ten programs, agencies and organizations that are helping women entrepreneurs be better represented in the ranks of American business owners.
-   [SBA Small Business Development Centers](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/find/?type=Small%20Business%20Development%20Center&pageNumber=1), which can help companies of all kinds locate additional funding opportunities at the national and local level.
-   [SCORE](https://www.score.org/), which can assign a volunteer business mentor who can provide guidance on numerous topics, including funding and operations.
