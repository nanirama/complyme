Embarking on a new project can be exciting and challenging in equal measure. With the right
preparation and guidance, your venture is poised for success. The ScrewFast First Project
Checklist is designed to provide a clear and structured approach to ensure you're
well-prepared every step of the way.

## Project Preparation

<ChecklistCardGrid>
  <ChecklistCard title="Scope and Objectives">
    <ChecklistItem>Ensure clarity of the project's scope, deliverables, and desired outcomes</ChecklistItem>
    <ChecklistItem>Define clear objectives and success criteria for project completion</ChecklistItem>
  </ChecklistCard>

  <ChecklistCard title="Tools and Equipment">
    <ChecklistItem>Make a list of all required ScrewFast tools and equipment</ChecklistItem>
    <ChecklistItem>Verify inventory availability and condition before starting</ChecklistItem>
  </ChecklistCard>

  <ChecklistCard title="Arranging Services">
    <ChecklistItem>If additional expertise or manpower is needed, arrange for ScrewFast construction services in advance</ChecklistItem>
    <ChecklistItem>Clarify service level agreements and timelines with your ScrewFast representative</ChecklistItem>
  </ChecklistCard>
</ChecklistCardGrid>

## Project Execution

### Initial Phase

<Steps>
  <Step>Set up the project site according to ScrewFast setup guidelines</Step>
  <Step>Conduct an initial team meeting to align on project goals and ScrewFast methodologies</Step>
  <Step>Establish project checkpoints and milestones for regular assessment</Step>
</Steps>
